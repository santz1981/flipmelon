# Flipmelon branding

**This is a proposed branding policy for review by the project owner before publication.**

The Flipmelon brand name, wordmark, icon, and original visual artwork are provided for use within and when accurately identifying the official Flipmelon project. Do not imply endorsement by the project owner for independently modified builds.

The MIT license applies to the project's original software source code. It is not intended as a general trademark license for creating unrelated products under the Flipmelon name. Third-party artwork or libraries may have separate terms.

Before publishing this repository, the owner should explicitly decide whether original artwork is also licensed under an open content license (for example CC BY 4.0) or remains all-rights-reserved, and document that decision here. The copyright ownership and trademark status have not been independently verified.
